Our Story

We built Robin's Nest Egg because of one very small, very loud reason: a daughter who didn't ask for a website, but is getting one anyway.

Somewhere between the first sleepless nights and the growing pile of nursery admin, it became obvious that nobody had made the thing she'll soon want — somewhere cheerful and completely independent, where children could learn about money and investing without a bank's logo watching over their shoulder.

So we're building it ourselves, one piece at a time, with a friendly robin as our guide (robins build nests, nests hold eggs, eggs turn into... well, you get it). It won't be perfect on day one. But it's 100% free, there are no adverts and no accounts, and it'll grow up right alongside the small person who inspired it.

Explore Money World

Money World is a map of eleven places to explore — from what money actually is, to earning it, saving it, growing it, and what it means to own a slice of a company.

Every topic is written three ways, so it meets each child where they are: Hatchlings (ages 5–6), Fledglings (ages 7–8) and High Flyers (ages 9–10).

Ten of the eleven zones are open, running all the way from What Is Money? through to Money Safety — including scams, borrowing, inflation and the money hidden inside games. Only the last zone is still to come.

There's also Money Words — short, plain-English explanations of tricky words like interest, inflation and tax, each written three ways. Useful when a question arrives out of nowhere.
