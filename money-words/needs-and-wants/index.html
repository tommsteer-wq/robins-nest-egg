Needs and wants

Things you must have, versus things that would be nice.

Three ways to explain it

🥚 Hatchlings · Ages 5–6

Needs are things you can’t do without, like food. Wants are things that would be lovely, like toys.

🐣 Fledglings · Ages 7–8

Needs keep you safe and well — food, a home, warm clothes. Wants are everything else. Both matter, but needs go first.

🐦 High Flyers · Ages 9–10

A way of sorting spending by how essential it is. The tricky part is that the line moves depending on your circumstances, and being honest about which is which is harder than it sounds.
