Profit

What’s left when you sell something for more than it cost you.

Three ways to explain it

🥚 Hatchlings · Ages 5–6

If you make something for 50p and sell it for £1, the extra 50p is your profit.

🐣 Fledglings · Ages 7–8

The money left over after you’ve paid for everything it took to make or buy the thing you sold.

🐦 High Flyers · Ages 9–10

What remains once every cost has been paid. It’s the number that decides whether a business can keep going — and it’s very different from how much money came in.
