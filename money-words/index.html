Money Words

Money words explained in plain English — three ways each, so you can pick whichever fits the child asking. Handy when a question arrives and you’d rather not say “I’ll explain when you’re older”.

🐦 Ten words so far. We add more whenever there’s time. If there’s a word you’d like explaining, do tell us.

  1. Budget A plan for your money.
  2. Compound interest When your interest starts earning interest of its own.
  3. Debt Money you owe someone else.
  4. Inflation When things slowly cost more than they used to.
  5. Interest Money a bank pays you for keeping your money there — or money you pay for borrowing.
  6. Investing Using money to buy something you hope will be worth more later.
  7. Needs and wants Things you must have, versus things that would be nice.
  8. Saving Keeping money instead of spending it now.
  9. Share One small piece of a company that you can own.
  10. Tax Money everyone pays towards the things we all share.
