Pocket money

Money some children are given regularly by a grown-up.

Three ways to explain it

🥚 Hatchlings · Ages 5–6

Money that some grown-ups give to children now and then, to spend or save as they like. Not every family does this.

🐣 Fledglings · Ages 7–8

A regular amount some children get — weekly or monthly — to practise making their own money decisions. Plenty of families do it differently, or not at all, and that’s completely normal.

🐦 High Flyers · Ages 9–10

A small regular income given to a child, usually so they can practise choosing, saving and going without. There’s no standard amount and no correct way to do it — it depends entirely on what a family can manage.
