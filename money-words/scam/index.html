Scam

A trick designed to take someone’s money.

Three ways to explain it

🥚 Hatchlings · Ages 5–6

When someone tries to trick you out of your money by pretending. If something feels wrong, tell a grown-up.

🐣 Fledglings · Ages 7–8

A trick designed to get your money or your information, usually by pretending to be someone they’re not. The best test: if it sounds too good to be true, it almost always is.

🐦 High Flyers · Ages 9–10

A deliberate deception designed to extract money or personal details. Scams nearly always rely on two things — an offer that seems unusually good, and pressure to act quickly. Slowing down and checking is the single best defence there is.
