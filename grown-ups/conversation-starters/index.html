Money conversation starters

Twenty questions for the car, the walk to school, or the ten minutes after dinner when nobody wants to get up yet.

🐦 These aren’t a quiz. There are no right answers, and the best conversations happen when a grown-up doesn’t know the answer either. One question is plenty for one journey.

1. What is money?

  • If money didn’t exist, how would we get bread?
  • What’s the oddest thing you can imagine people using as money?

2. Earning it

  • What job would you pick if every job paid exactly the same?
  • How many people did a job today to get our dinner onto this table?

3. Saving it

  • What’s the longest you’ve ever waited for something you really wanted?
  • Was it worth the wait? Would you do it again?

4. Growing it

  • £1 now, or £5 next year. Which, and why?
  • Where do you think a bank actually keeps our money?

5. Own a slice

  • If you could own a tiny piece of any company, which one?
  • What would make you want to sell it again?

6. Spending it well

  • Best thing you’ve ever spent money on? Worst?
  • That advert we just saw — what did it want you to feel?

7. Borrowing it

  • If you borrowed £5 from me, what would be a fair amount to pay back?
  • Why would a bank lend money to somebody it has never met?

8. Prices go up

  • Ask the oldest person you know what a chocolate bar cost when they were little.
  • If everything costs more next year, does that mean we’re poorer?

9. Giving & sharing

  • If somebody handed you £10 to give away, who would get it?
  • Is giving your time as good as giving money?

10. Money safety

  • What would make you suspicious of a message?
  • How much real money is sitting in your game account right now?

Where these come from

Each one matches a zone of Money World, so if a question starts something and you want to keep going, the zone with the same name has the answer written three ways.
